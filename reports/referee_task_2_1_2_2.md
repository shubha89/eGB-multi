## Definitions to use in the revision

For real A/E time series, define the complex correlation

$$
C(a,b)=4\sum_{c\in\{A,E\}}\int_B \frac{\tilde a_c(f)\tilde b_c^*(f)}{S_c(f)}\,df,
\qquad (a|b)_{AE}=\operatorname{Re}C(a,b).
$$

The normalized overlap is

$$
\mathcal O(a,b)=\frac{(a|b)_{AE}}{\sqrt{(a|a)_{AE}(b|b)_{AE}}}.
$$

It is signed and has no implied parameter maximization. An absolute value of a real overlap and the magnitude of a complex correlation are distinct operations.

The match maximizes the overlap over the specified relative phase/time degrees of freedom. The fitting factor maximizes further over the independent parameters of the stated circular family and search domain. For this long-lived circular model, reference epoch is a coordinate choice once carrier frequency and phase are consistently transformed; the detector's absolute observation times stay fixed. Phase is explicitly maximized. Any approximation to the broader template maximum must be labelled by the parameters and domain actually searched.

The existing code computes

$$
Q_{\rm legacy}=\max_{\dot f\in G}\frac{|C(d,h_{\dot f})|}{\sqrt{(d|d)_{AE}(h_{\dot f}|h_{\dot f})_{AE}}},
$$

where $G$ is the nine-point frequency-derivative grid and the remaining circular parameters are fixed. Use the name **legacy restricted complex-correlation score** for archived results. It is not the fitting factor over the full circular family. The manuscript's real-inner-product equation also does not exactly describe this complex-magnitude calculation. Definitions reference: [Afle et al., Section IV](https://arxiv.org/html/1803.07695v2).

### Physical phase check

Circular templates at fixed remaining parameters can be written as

$$
h(\phi_0+\delta)=R+C_0\cos(2\delta)+S_0\sin(2\delta).
$$

The diagnostic obtains $R,C_0,S_0$ from three locally generated eGB circular responses and evaluates the full real A/E Gram matrix, including unequal quadrature norms, nonorthogonal quadratures, and the phase-independent term. It samples the entire phase period and refines the best local peaks. Only phase is maximized; frequency, derivative, sky, and orientation stay fixed.

For the five selected legacy candidates, explicit physical phase maximization differs from the legacy complex-magnitude score by at most **2.0386e-8**. An additional explicitly generated phase-shifted TDI waveform agrees with its basis reconstruction to **6.0561e-12** in amplitude-fitted weighted relative residual. At 0.1 mHz, the quadrature norm ratio is 0.998551 and the normalized cross inner product is 8.251e-4, so orthogonal/equal-norm quadratures cannot be assumed exactly.

These checks show that the selected legacy results already approximate phase maximization closely. They do not establish exact phase maximization for every source, or replace frequency, derivative, sky, and orientation optimization. See [phase_convention_audit.json](../outputs/referee/task_2_1_2_2_20261007/phase_convention_audit.json).

### Noise and unit conventions requiring follow-up

The current `benchmarks/lisainstrument_welch_psd.py` calls `simulate_lisainstrument_tdi_noise` without specifying TDI generation. That local function defaults to generation 1, whereas the comparison uses generation 2. The cached PSD CSV does not record its generation, so its actual historical setting remains unconfirmed. Task 2.3 must establish a PSD with recorded, compatible signal/noise TDI conventions before revised physical interpretations.

The source link formula returns dimensionless fractional-frequency GW response, and `pytdi_data_from_links` assigns those arrays directly to science measurements. The noise generator uses instrument frequency fluctuations in Hz. A global amplitude-unit conversion cancels from normalized overlaps and relative residuals, but absolute norms cannot be interpreted as physical SNR until signal/PSD units are matched. Record the conversion explicitly in Task 2.3. TDI-generation differences can change frequency weighting and are not removable by a global amplitude rescaling.

## Task 2.2 circular family and parameter contract

Use `eccentricity=0`, conservative `physics_mode="1pn_periastron"`, and `evolution_mode="fixed"` through `eccentric_links_jax`. In this branch, **fixed means prescribed source evolution rather than Peters–Mathews evolution**: a nonzero supplied `fdot` still produces a chirp.

Let $t_{\rm ref}$ be the midpoint of the fixed observation interval. In this branch,

$$
\omega(t)=\pi[f_{\rm ref}+\dot f(t-t_{\rm ref})],\qquad
\phi(t)=\phi_{\rm ref}+\pi f_{\rm ref}(t-t_{\rm ref})+\frac{\pi\dot f}{2}(t-t_{\rm ref})^2.
$$

The dominant GW phase is $2\phi(t)$. Thus `mean_motion/pi` is the azimuthal GW carrier frequency, and `phi0` is orbital phase. For an eccentric 1PN injection, radial mean motion differs from azimuthal frequency; harmonic seeds must use the package's actual sideband frequencies rather than treating every line as an exact half-integer multiple of the azimuthal carrier.

| Quantity | Treatment | Initial domain or convention |
|---|---|---|
| Carrier frequency $f_{\rm ref}$ | Search continuously in each relevant harmonic basin | $[0.2 f_{\rm inj},10 f_{\rm inj}]$ Hz for the initial per-injection domain; verify bandwidth and boundary expansion before production. |
| Frequency derivative $\dot f$ | Search continuously and independently of masses | $[-b,b]$ Hz/s, with $b=\max[64\dot f_{\rm GR,inj},\,2(f_{\rm high}/f_{\rm inj})|\dot f_{\rm PM,inj}|,\,4/T^2]$. Require positive instantaneous frequency at all retarded evaluation times. |
| Ecliptic latitude $\beta$ | Search | $[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$. |
| Ecliptic longitude $\lambda$ | Search | $[0,2\pi)$, periodic. |
| Inclination $\iota$ | Vary or maximize using a validated physical amplitude parametrization | $\cos\iota\in[-1,1]$. |
| Polarization angle $\psi$ | Vary or maximize using a validated physical amplitude parametrization | $[0,\pi)$, periodic. |
| Orbital phase $\phi_{\rm ref}$ | Maximize explicitly or using a validated phase basis | $[0,\pi)$; equivalent to a full $[0,2\pi)$ GW-phase period. |
| Common amplitude / distance | Profile analytically; cancels from normalized overlap | Positive common scale across A and E. Reference source distance remains 1 kpc. |
| Component masses $m_1,m_2$ | Retain in the exact 1PN chirping reference family | $0.1\le m_2\le m_1\le1.4\ M_\odot$; this is a computational WD-like domain, not a population prior. |
| Reference epoch $t_0$ | Fix as a coordinate convention | Observation midpoint; transform frequency and phase together. |
| Eccentricity | Fix | Exactly zero. |
| Detector epoch, orbit, TDI, weighting and band | Fixed within a comparison | Common settings for injection and every candidate; band and injection norm must not depend on candidate frequency. |

The machine-readable [circular_parameter_contract.json](../outputs/referee/task_2_1_2_2_20261007/circular_parameter_contract.json) records these choices for the five rerun injections. The bounds are initial finite analysis domains. They must be expanded/tested in Task 2.6, and the band must pass Task 2.8/Comment 6 convergence. In particular, a 3 mHz injection's proposed 30 mHz upper band cannot be evaluated at the legacy 10 mHz Nyquist frequency.

The usual eight circular source quantities are frequency, frequency derivative, two sky angles, amplitude, inclination, polarization, and phase. The existing exact chirping 1PN implementation has additional weak mass-dependent amplitude-shape terms. The reference contract therefore retains both masses instead of asserting an exact eight-parameter reduction prematurely. Neither the eccentric model's 11-dimensional parameter count nor a raw dataclass field count establishes the number of independent circular degrees of freedom.

### Circular mass dependence

For the implemented 1PN circular limit, write

$$
x=(GM\omega/c^3)^{2/3},\quad q=1+(-1+\eta/3)x,\quad
r=\frac{GM}{c^2x}q,\quad \dot r=0,\quad \dot\phi=\omega.
$$

The polarization coefficients reduce to

$$
a=c^2x(q^{-1}+q^2),\qquad c_r=c^2x(q^{-1}-q^2),\qquad b=0.
$$

At zero prescribed chirp, $c_r$ is constant and its contribution cancels in the one-way link time difference. The remaining circular response depends on mass only through a common amplitude at fixed frequency/orientation. With nonzero chirp, $x(t)$ changes and the mass dependence is not mathematically an exact common scale.

For a full-year 3 mHz circular response at inclination 0.8, the audit varied masses from the reference $0.5+0.5\,M_\odot$ to $0.8+0.6$, $1.0+0.3$, $1.4+0.1$, and $1.4+1.4\,M_\odot$. After a common amplitude fit, the largest weighted relative residual is **2.4009e-15** for zero chirp and **2.3869e-11** for $\dot f=32\dot f_{\rm GR,ref}=2.6042e-15$ Hz/s. This suggests that a reduced mass-fixed search may be practical, but reduction across the full candidate domain requires the later error-budget checks. See [mass_shape_audit.csv](../outputs/referee/task_2_1_2_2_20261007/mass_shape_audit.csv).

The physical orientation constraints also matter: a time-varying $c_r$ term is tied to inclination and polarization. An unrestricted four-amplitude construction must not silently replace the exact family unless its approximation error is quantified. Both A and E must use the same source coefficients.

### Reference epoch and backend checks

For a circular fixed-JAX waveform, moving $t_0$ to $t_0+\Delta$ requires

$$
f'_0=f_0+\dot f\Delta,\qquad
\phi'_0=\phi_0+\pi f_0\Delta+\frac{\pi\dot f}{2}\Delta^2.
$$

Orbital phase can then be reduced modulo $\pi$. With detector timestamps unchanged, the full-year TDI waveform is preserved to **1.1821e-10** in amplitude-fitted weighted relative residual in the tested chirping case. Doubling distance produces exactly zero amplitude-fitted residual at recorded precision. Therefore epoch and distance do not introduce extra independent normalized-overlap search directions in this defined family. This transformation is not a prescription for shifting a detector time series or reinitializing a Peters–Mathews trajectory.

The NumPy `eccentric_dynamics` fixed branch ignores the dataclass `fdot` field. For the tested prescribed chirp, its source waveform differs from the JAX chirping waveform by relative L2 **1.2011**, while agreeing with zero-chirp JAX at **2.5585e-16**. Consequently the circular prescribed-chirp runs must use the fixed JAX path. The exact-link Peters–Mathews fallback rejects nonzero prescribed `fdot`; retain `fdot=0` on evolved eccentric injections. See [epoch_and_backend_audit.json](../outputs/referee/task_2_1_2_2_20261007/epoch_and_backend_audit.json).

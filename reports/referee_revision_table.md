## Primary-source checks for the other comments

The referee report was re-read on 12 October. The following primary-source identities were verified; Tasks 3 and 4's focused comparisons and Task 5's scope assessment are now saved. Manuscript edits and any new matched-configuration quantitative cross-model comparison remain pending.

- [Moore et al., arXiv:2310.06568](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06568): multi-harmonic eccentric LISA analysis, Bayesian eccentricity inference, periastron precession and harmonic/source misidentification. Relevant to Comments 3 and 4; distinguish its measurability observable from circular-template SNR loss.
- [Lindblom, Owen and Brown, arXiv:0809.3844](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.3844), [Baird et al., arXiv:1211.0546](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.0546), and [Vallisneri, arXiv:1207.4759](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.4759): noise-norm accuracy, regular Fisher confidence-region and high-SNR nested-model comparison frameworks checked for Task 3. Their coefficients/statistical calibrations are not automatically eccentricity-detection thresholds.
- [Yunes et al., arXiv:0906.0313](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.0313) and [Tanay, Haney and Gopakumar, arXiv:1602.03081](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.03081): established Fourier-domain eccentric constructions; the latter includes PN eccentricity evolution. Do not imply these physical ingredients are exclusive to a time-domain implementation.
- [Xuan et al., arXiv:2605.15265](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15265): eccentric stellar-mass BBHs and harmonic confusion in LISA. It is contextual support from a different source population, not a direct DWD numerical benchmark.
- [Klein et al., arXiv:1801.08542](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.08542) and [Klein, arXiv:2106.10291](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.10291): FD eccentric/precessing constructions with secular evolution; the latter explicitly implements a rigid-adiabatic LISA XYZ TDI response and is Moore's cited response reference. Tanay's FD model separately leaves precession-induced harmonic structure untreated. See the [scoped capability comparison](referee_task_4_literature_novelty.md).
- [Willems, Vecchio and Kalogera, arXiv:0706.3700](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.3700): tidal/rotational contributions to DWD apsidal motion. The cited frequency statement is about **orbital frequency**, which must not be silently substituted for the manuscript's carrier-frequency convention.
- [Xuan, Peng and Chen, arXiv:2012.00049](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.00049): tertiary acceleration and chirp-mass degeneracy in DWDs. Relevant to model scope and interpretation, not an effect implemented by the present isolated Peters model.
